08 / The Rigor Charter

Bold vision.
Visible evidence boundaries.

Perception Models Tools, Not Oracles

Pretrained Models are Representational Tools

AION provides multimodal astronomical embeddings. AstroM3 and FALCO address time-series representations. Their reported task accuracy does not demonstrate calibrated cosmology reliability in this proposed benchmark.

AION ↗ AstroM3 ↗ FALCO ↗
Evaluation Security Zero-Leakage

The Sealed Evaluator Vault

Ground truth, injection amplitudes, random seeds, and test labels are sealed outside the analyst environment. Evaluator truth never leaks through filenames, logs, tool outputs, or context windows.

Sid's Research ↗ BU & IAIFI ↗
Simulations Community Tested

Community Infrastructure First

SNANA provides proven forward simulation and analysis pipelines. This project does not depend on a differentiable rewrite or custom simulators. Black-box adversarial search begins within measured compute budgets.
